This Privacy Policy describes how GRIP (Geopolitical Risk Interpretation Platform) (the "Application") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you access or use the Application.

The Application is designed to perform geopolitical analysis based on analytical models and user-defined inputs. Respect for user privacy is a fundamental principle of the Application's design.

By using the Application, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

Application preferences and settings;

The Application does not require account registration.

Automatically Collected Information:

Depending on the platform through which the Application is distributed, limited technical information may be collected automatically, including:

device model;

operating system version;

application version;

crash reports;

purchase verification information;

technical diagnostic information required for application stability.

Such information is collected by the operating system or the official application marketplace and may not be directly accessible to the Licensor.

Information We Do Not Collect:

The Application does not intentionally collect:

precise location data;

contacts;

photographs;

microphone recordings;

camera images;

advertising identifiers for personalized advertising;

biometric information;

health information;

financial payment credentials.

3. How Information Is Used

Information processed by the Application is used solely to:

perform geopolitical calculations;

store user-created profiles;

maintain application functionality;
