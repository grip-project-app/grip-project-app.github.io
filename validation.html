Validation

Testing consistency without claiming certainty

GRIP is not validated by asking whether it can predict a single political decision. It is tested by checking whether the same inputs produce stable calculations, whether custom profiles behave plausibly against comparable cases, and whether historical analogues expose strengths or calibration limits.

Four layers of validation

1

Full regression testing

Every standard-country and standard-scenario pair is recalculated and compared with the established baseline.

658 standard combinations recalculated
2

Custom-profile calibration

Own Country and Own Scenario profiles are tested against comparable standard cases to identify close analogues, broad consistency and material divergence.

59 reproducible calibration cases
3

Historical plausibility assessment

Three real countries were recreated as custom country profiles and paired with three custom scenarios. For each case, a comparable recent historical event was identified and the GRIP result was compared with the state's documented reaction.

3 documented historical comparison cases
4

Application output verification

Selected cases were checked independently to confirm that the calculation is generated correctly and transferred into the application without changing the expected output fields.

Module 3 — Historical plausibility assessment

Three real countries were recreated through the Own Country function, and three custom scenarios were constructed through the Own Scenario function. For each country–scenario pair, a comparable recent real-world event was identified. The GRIP output was then compared with the documented external reaction, internal response and rhetoric of the state concerned.

Module 4 — Application output verification

After the model calculations had been calibrated, selected cases were checked independently. The expected values were compared field by field with the results generated inside the application. This verifies that the calculation logic is transferred correctly into the implemented GRIP workflow and that the application presents the intended outputs.

What validation establishes

Validation establishes calculation stability, reproducibility and analytical plausibility within the tested scope. It does not convert GRIP into a forecasting oracle. The model remains an interpretation framework whose results depend on the quality of its country and scenario inputs.
