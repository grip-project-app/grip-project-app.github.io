Structural Constraints

The visible result is built from external reaction, internal pressure, internal response, rhetoric and signal level. These categories are not independent. Mathematical and logical relationships prevent internally contradictory combinations.

× Low internal pressure cannot produce a repressive internal response.

× The highest signal level cannot coexist with conditions defined as minimally pressured.

The unrestricted category system contains 1,280 combinations. Direct analysis of the current calculation logic reduces the theoretical maximum to no more than 592 structurally possible patterns. Computational reachability testing has already produced valid input witnesses for 572 of them; the remaining narrow threshold cases are being examined separately before a final reachable total is published.

Transparent Analytical Design

GRIP intentionally reduces an enormous analytical space into a limited number of interpretable geopolitical outcome patterns. The objective is not to maximise the number of possible outputs, but to maximise analytical consistency while keeping the result understandable for human interpretation.

The conceptual structure and factor categories can be explained openly. Exact calibration weights, threshold values and the detailed implementation of individual modifiers remain part of the internal calculation model.
